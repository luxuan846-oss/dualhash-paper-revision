\section{Conclusion and Discussion}
\label{Section: Conclusion}
This paper proposed DualHash, a stochastic primal-dual algorithm for deep hashing with rigorous non-asymptotic \blue{oracle complexity} guarantees. Our approach addressed the optimization challenge of W-type regularization with nonlinear composition through a ``decoupling-dual-structure'' strategy: \blue{reformulating} the nonconvex regularizer \blue{through its convex conjugate, which yields} closed-form proximal updates. \blue{We showed that the resulting min--max problem captures the stationarity of the original deep hashing objective up to the quantization error, and} established complexity bounds of $\mathcal{O}(\varepsilon^{-4})$ for DualHash-StoM and improved $\mathcal{O}(\varepsilon^{-3})$ for DualHash-StoRM\blue{, which yield near-stationarity guarantees for the original objective}. Extensive experiments showed that DualHash achieved the highest mAP at 32 bits or more on CIFAR-10 and NUS-WIDE and at 48 and 64 bits on ImageNet-100, together with the lowest quantization errors across bit lengths. 

Beyond deep hashing, our core \textbf{decoupling-dual-structure} optimization principle offered a promising pathway for tackling nonconvex regularization challenges in related domains, including image \blue{reconstruction} \cite{ding2020ldct_pfbs}, plug-and-play proximal methods \cite{JMLR:v23:20-1297} and binary neural networks \cite{bai2019proxquant}.
