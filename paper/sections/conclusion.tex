\section{Conclusion and Discussion}
\label{Section: Conclusion}
This paper proposed DualHash, a stochastic primal-dual algorithm for deep hashing with rigorous non-asymptotic \blue{oracle complexity} guarantees. Our approach addressed the optimization challenge of W-type regularization with nonlinear composition through a ``decoupling-dual-structure'' strategy: \blue{moving} the nonconvex regularizer \blue{to the} dual space with closed-form proximal updates. \blue{We showed that the resulting min--max problem captures the stationarity of the original deep hashing objective up to the quantization error, and} established complexity bounds of $\mathcal{O}(\varepsilon^{-4})$ for DualHash-StoM and improved $\mathcal{O}(\varepsilon^{-3})$ for DualHash-StoRM\blue{, which yield near-stationarity guarantees for the original objective}. Extensive experiments validated that DualHash achieved superior retrieval performance across all metrics, including the highest mAP at 32 bits or more
ed{, except for the 32-bit ImageNet-100 setting}, consistently lower quantization errors, and strong robustness on all three datasets (CIFAR-10, NUS-WIDE, and ImageNet-100).
